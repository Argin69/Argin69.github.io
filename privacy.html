Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

ReminderBot is a personal reminder assistant used by one person, its owner. It runs on the owner's own laptop. This page explains what it does with data, including data from Google.

Google Calendar

  • What it asks for: permission to manage calendar events (calendar.events) in the owner's own Google account. It does not ask for email, contacts, files or anything else.
  • What it does with it: it creates an event for each reminder, and moves or deletes that same event when the reminder is moved or cancelled. It does not read, copy or analyse other events in the calendar.
  • Where it is kept: the sign-in token from Google and the IDs of the events it created are stored only on the owner's laptop. Nothing is stored on any other server.
  • Sharing: data from Google is never sold, shared with anyone, used for advertising, or sent to an AI model.

ReminderBot's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Notes and messages

The owner's notes arrive through Telegram and are stored only on the owner's laptop. To understand a note, its text is sent to Claude (by Anthropic) through the owner's own Claude account. Secrets the owner marks as secret are encrypted on the laptop and never sent to Claude or Telegram.

Removing access and data

The owner can remove ReminderBot's access to Google Calendar at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the ReminderBot folder and its encrypted backup files on the laptop deletes all of its data.
