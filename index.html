ReminderBot

A personal reminder assistant for one person. It is not a public service and nobody else can sign up or use it.

What it does

  • Its owner writes short notes to it in Telegram, in English or Burmese (including Burmese written in English letters).
  • It understands notes like "tomorrow 8 AM doctor" and turns them into reminders.
  • It sends a reminder message in Telegram at the right time.
  • It adds each reminder to the owner's own Google Calendar as an event, so the phone shows it too. When the owner moves or cancels a reminder, it moves or deletes that event.
  • It remembers notes the owner asks it to keep, and answers questions about them.

Where it runs

ReminderBot runs only on its owner's own laptop. There is no ReminderBot server, website account or app store listing.
